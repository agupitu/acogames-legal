Privacy Policy — Volt Sync

Last updated: October 29, 2026 · Español

Controller: Aco Games · Contact: platephoenix.design@gmail.com

What data the app handles

  • Game progress (levels, stars, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not send it to any server. It is deleted when you reset progress in Settings or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): to show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect the device advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data. Google processes this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and other regions where required) we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing): if you buy "Remove ads", Google Play processes the payment. We only receive the purchase confirmation; we do not access your payment details.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for accounts, names, email addresses or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the camera, microphone or contacts.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

You can limit personalized advertising in your Android settings (Google → Ads) and withdraw your consent from within the app. For any question, write to platephoenix.design@gmail.com.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version at this same address.
